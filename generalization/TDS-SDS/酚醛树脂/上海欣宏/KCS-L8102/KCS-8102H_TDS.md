# HiRENOL® KCS-8102H 技术数据表（TDS）

## 产品概述

HIRENOL® KCS-8102H 是采用特殊工艺生产的热反应性树脂，适用于工业设备、热交换器、桶或灌装涂层等恶劣作业场所，该产品具有低BPA含量、固化快、交联度高等优点。

（英文版描述：HIRENOL® KCS-8102H resin is a special, heat-reactive resole resin designed for severe operating environments, such as industrial equipment, heat exchangers, drum and pail linings. The resin product have the properties of low BPA content, quickly cured and high degree of crosslinking.）

## 储存

在阴凉、干燥、密封处存放；在25℃条件下保质期为6个月。

存放处远离火源和阳光直射，在使用前请仔细阅读MSDS，按照安全操作规范进行。

（英文版说明：Keep in cool, dry, well ventilate condition and in closed containers. We recommend a serving temperature of 25°C below. And the shelf life is 6 months. Keep away from sources and direct sunlight. Recommended safe handling procedures are discussed in the information on the MSDS should reviewed and understood before working.）

## 包装

按照客户要求，可采用 200Kg 桶包装，特殊订单可变更包装。

（英文版说明：The standard package for KCS-8102H is a drum containing 200 Kg net of resin. A package can be changed with special order.）
