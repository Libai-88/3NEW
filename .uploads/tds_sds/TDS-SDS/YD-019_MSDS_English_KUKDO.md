## 4. First Aid Measures

**Eye contact:** Flush eyes with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes while holding eyelids open. Consult a physician immediately.

**Skin contact:** Immediately remove contaminated clothing or shoes, wash skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Consult a physician if irritation persists.

**Inhalation:** Move person to fresh air area and provide oxygen if breathing is difficult. Consult a physician immediately.

**Ingestion:** Do not induce vomiting. Consult a physician immediately.

**Acute and Delayed Symptoms:**

| Route | Short-term Exposure | Long-term Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Inhalation | Irritation, vomiting, difficult breathing, headache, sleepiness | May cause paralysis or spasm |
| Ingestion | Vomiting, diarrhea, irregular beating | No data for bad effect |
| Skin contact | Irritation, allergic reaction | Same effect as short-term exposure |
| Eye contact | May cause irritation | Same effect as short-term exposure |

## 10. Stability and Reactivity

**Chemical stability:** Stable at normal temperature and pressure.
**Hazardous reactions:** May cause exothermic polymerization with acid, amines or other epoxy.
**Conditions to avoid:** Avoid contact with strong oxidizing agent, heat, spark and flame. Don't apply heat to container.
**Incompatible materials:** Acid, amine, anhydride, oxidizing agent, chloroform, chloroform acid.
**Hazardous decomposition products:** May produce hazardous carbon oxides, chloro hydrogen.
